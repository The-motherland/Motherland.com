What we never do

We never sell, share or rent personal information. We use no advertising, fingerprinting or third-party analytics on this site.

Your scripts and ideas

Anything you share with us for a project remains yours. We sign a confidentiality agreement before reading any pitch, and we do not use client material to train AI models.

How we use AI responsibly

We disclose AI use in our productions, we do not imitate real people without written consent, and a human reviews every final frame.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, request correction or deletion, and withdraw consent at any time. Because this site stores nothing on our servers, deleting your browser data erases it all.
